Hi, I’m **Hassan Kohla** — a **Mechanical Engineering researcher** and **Teaching Assistant** at the Egyptian Russian University (ERU).  
My research merges **Humidification–Dehumidification (HDH) desalination** with **Phase Change Materials (PCMs)** to reduce compressor duty cycles, cut energy use, and lower cost per liter of fresh water.  
I design and build complete experimental rigs, collect high-resolution data, and develop smooth theoretical models to match the results within ±10 %.

---

## 🔬 Research Focus
- **HDH desalination with PCM integration** (water & paraffin/graphene)  
- **Thermal system control & duty-cycle optimization** (compressor/fans)  
- **Heat transfer in concentric-tube condensers & evaporators (R-134a)**  
- **Sensor networks & data acquisition** (DS18B20 arrays, humidity, flow)  
- **PV cooling using TEC modules** (dynamic vs fixed arrays)

---

## 🧪 Current Projects
- **Dual-PCM HDH Rig** — Stainless steel duct (30 × 30 × 10 cm) containing condenser & evaporator (10 concentric tube pairs), R-134a loop, ultrasonic humidifiers, axial fans, and Arduino-based control and logging.  
- **Theoretical vs Experimental Model Overlay** — Minute-resolution (≈240 min) comparisons of experimental and smoothed theoretical curves within ±10 %.  
- **PV Cooling Experiments** — TEC array layouts, step-up converters (XL4015, LM2596), and temperature monitoring for efficiency retention in hot climates.
